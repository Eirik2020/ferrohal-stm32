## Finding 1 (High): `start_windowed` reset the chip at once

**Measured.** Scratch firmware on the Nucleo-H753ZI, deleted afterwards, kept its
progress in one doubleword of `.uninit` RAM. `Watchdog::start_windowed(100 ms, 30 ms)`
returned, and a watchdog reset followed within 1 ms, before any feed: two of two runs.
The same firmware writing the registers in RM0433 45.3.3's order (enable, unlock,
PR, RLR, wait for SR = 0, WINR, no `0xAAAA`) survived 20 feeds 85 ms after each
reload, every time.

**Cause (from the code and RM0433 45.3.3).** `start_with` wrote WINR before the
prescaler and reload settled, never waited for WVU, and ended with a `0xAAAA` key
reload. Writing WINR already reloads the counter, so that key reload came with the
counter at its reload value, above the window: a refresh outside the window, which
resets the chip.

**Why the bench passed.** Run K counted any watchdog reset after its early feed as
IWDG-005, and the reset it saw was the start's own. No requirement said a feed inside
the window must survive. Today's run K log also printed the IWDG-005 PASS in the same
boot as the starvation report. Its marker was an untagged single word in `.uninit`,
so a leftover value plus the starvation's own watchdog reset read as a pass without
the window ever being started. The leftover was probably the scratch firmware's;
that is inferred.

**Fixed in `dd41cbd`.**
- `src/iwdg.rs`: wait for SR to clear (PVU, RVU and, on `iwdg_v2`, WVU), write WINR
  last, wait for WVU, and send no key reload after it, as ST's HAL does. The path
  without a window is unchanged in effect.
- IWDG-007 (`src/requirements/iwdg.yaml`): a watchdog started with a window shall
  not reset the chip when fed inside the window.
- Run K (`hil/nucleo-h753zi-loopback/src/bin/run_k.rs`) now runs three boots.
  - Boot 2 feeds five times 85 ms after each reload (inside the window, which opens
    at about 71 ms and closes at about 101 ms at the board's 31.7 kHz), then three
    more while the host drains RTT, then once 10 ms after a reload, above the window.
  - Progress and boot 2's failures cross the resets in one magic-tagged doubleword.
    Boot 3 reports first, and only after a watchdog reset.
  - A failure leaves the watchdog to end the run instead of exiting into a fresh
    run K. Waits use the cycle counter.
- Two details found on the bench while building the check. A key write straight
  after a feed counts as the same reload and did not reset, so the early feed waits
  10 ms. The IWDG-007 line was lost when the reset came 10 ms after printing it,
  hence the drain feeds.

**Evidence for the fix (measured).** `hil/run-h753.sh k` passed 9 of 9 checks in 7 of
8 runs. The eighth lost its probe session at a reset (`SwdApFault`) and had no failed
check. With the old `src/iwdg.rs` put back, run K fails
`fed inside the window: no reset [IWDG-007]`, so the check sees the defect.
`checks/run.sh` 21 of 21; `reqtool validate` at CI's pin `b28689a`: 118
requirements valid.

## Finding 2 (Medium): I2C accepts push-pull pins

`into_alternate` and `into_alternate_open_drain` return the same
`Pin<P, N, Alternate<AF>>`, so `i2c::Wiring` cannot refuse a push-pull SCL or SDA.
That is the defect the Pico bench found on every FerroWasp board (RESOLVED,
2026-11-08). Inferred from the code; recorded in DEBT, not fixed.
